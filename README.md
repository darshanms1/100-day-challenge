# 100-Day Challenge — Task Tracker

A dedicated, lightweight, and responsive task-tracking table application designed to keep you consistent across your **100-day journey** from **October 26, 2026 to February 2, 2027**.

---

## 📅 Challenge Timeline

- **Start Date:** Monday, October 26, 2026 (Day 1)
- **End Date:** Tuesday, February 2, 2027 (Day 100)
- **Total Duration:** 100 consecutive days

---

## 📋 The 5 Daily Task Columns (X-Axis)

Every single day includes exactly these five tasks:

1. **DSA** — Solve 2 questions
2. **MCQs** — Complete 10 MCQs
3. **English Practice** — 1 hour 30 minutes
4. **Project Development** — 2 hours
5. **Trading** — Complete 2 trading tasks

---

## ⚡ Key Features

- **Interactive Checkboxes:**
  - Click any checkbox to mark it complete (✅).
  - Click again to uncheck it (☐).
  - Full keyboard accessibility (press `Space` or `Enter` on any task cell).
- **Independent Date Tracking:**
  - Every day is tracked independently with its own state.
- **Automatic Auto-Save (`localStorage`):**
  - All task checkbox states are instantly preserved in your browser's local storage.
  - Refreshing or reopening the page retains all your progress seamlessly.
- **Daily Progress Calculation:**
  - Each date row displays real-time progress (`0/5`, `2/5`, `5/5`) with percentage and progress bar.
- **Dynamic Streak Counter:**
  - Streak advances when **all 5 tasks** for a day are completed.
  - Tracks both **Current Streak** and **Best Streak**.
- **Quick Jump to Today:**
  - Click **"🎯 Jump to Today"** to instantly scroll to and highlight today's row.
- **Filter & Search:**
  - Filter by *All Days*, *Fully Completed*, *In Progress*, or *Unstarted*.
  - Instant date search (e.g. search "Nov 14" or "Day 25").
- **Dark & Light Modes:**
  - Toggle between dark theme and light theme with preference saved automatically.
- **Backup & Restore:**
  - Export your progress to a `.json` file anytime.
  - Import your backup file on any other device or browser.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari):

- Double-click `index.html` in File Explorer, or
- Right-click `index.html` → Open with → Your preferred browser.
